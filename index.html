DOOR RULES & ETHICS

Admission to Social Euphoria is a privilege, not a commercial transaction. These 5 protocols are enforced by private security to preserve the intimacy, crowd safety, and acoustic purity of our space.

RULE 01 20:00 CUTOFF

Prompt 20:00 Guest List Cutoff

Complimentary or priority guest list privileges expire promptly at 20:00 IST. Arrive early to maintain your access tier. No exceptions for traffic or personal delays.

RULE 02 20:00–21:00 WINDOW

Cover Shift Protocol

Between 20:00 and 21:00, standard cover charges apply to all arrivals, including pre-approved profiles.

RULE 03 ABSOLUTE SEAL

Permanent 21:00 Gate Lock

At 21:00 IST, estate gates physically lock. No further admittance is permitted under any circumstances. Security personnel are legally instructed to refuse all late arrivals.

RULE 04 CURATED 1:1

Digital Profile Vetting

Every guest must hold an authenticated digital access dossier. Walk-ins and unvetted companions will be turned away. The door committee reserves absolute discretion.

RULE 05 DRESS CODE

Mandatory Thematic Attire

Each edition enforces a strict aesthetic mandate (e.g. Resort Chic for Sunset Soirées, All-White for The White Lotus, Neo-Nocturne for After Dark). Non-compliant attire will be rejected at the gate.
