The Rules of the Cult

Social Euphoria maintains an uncompromising door policy. Exclusivity isn't a marketing gimmick — it's the fundamental reason why our energy cannot be replicated elsewhere.

01
Curated Guest List Only
Request your invite through our Instagram portal. Guest profiles undergo individual verification by the organizing team before approval.
02
Strict 8:00 PM Arrival Window
Free and discounted guest list entry closes at 8:00 PM sharp. Any late arrival invalidates the guest list status without exception.
03
Paid Cover Gate Shift
Between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM, entry is subject to full paid cover at the door — strictly on a first-come, first-served basis subject to capacity.
04
Permanent Gate Lock at 9:00 PM
Gates close permanently at 9:00 PM. No late walk-ins, regardless of status. You are either inside the universe or outside.
05
Mandatory Theme Dress Code
Theme alignment is strictly enforced. Swimwear for pool takeovers, all-white for White Lotus, and neon rave aesthetic for After Dark.
