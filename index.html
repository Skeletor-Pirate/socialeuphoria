04 / THE CODE

The Protocol

Entry to Social Euphoria is a privilege, not a transaction. These five protocols are non-negotiable and strictly enforced by private security to preserve the intimacy, safety, and acoustic purity of our space.

CRITICAL GATE LOCK TIMELINE
DOORS OPEN: 19:00 IST
GUEST LIST CUTOFF: 20:00 IST
PERMANENT GATE LOCK: 21:00 IST (STRICT)
RULE 01 20:00 CUTOFF

Prompt 20:00 Guest List Expiration

Complimentary or priority guest list privileges expire precisely at 20:00 IST. No exceptions are granted for traffic or personal delays regardless of member tier.

RULE 02 20:00–21:00 WINDOW

Cover Shift Protocol

Between 20:00 and 21:00, standard venue cover charges apply to all arrivals, including pre-approved profiles. Arrive early to maintain your privileges.

RULE 03 ABSOLUTE LOCK

Permanent 21:00 Gate Lock

At 21:00 IST, estate gates physically lock. No further entry is permitted under any circumstances. Security personnel are legally instructed to refuse all late admittance.

RULE 04 CURATION VETTING

1:1 Profile Curation & Verification

Every guest must hold an authenticated digital access dossier. Walk-ins and unvetted companions are not admitted. The door committee reserves absolute discretion.

RULE 05 DRESS CODE

Mandatory Thematic Attire

Each edition enforces a strict aesthetic mandate (e.g. Resort Chic for Sunset Soirées, All-White for The White Lotus, Neo-Nocturne for After Dark). Non-compliant attire will be rejected at the gate.
