How It Works

The
Rules

Social Euphoria is not your average night out. We maintain a specific crowd dynamic. Exclusivity isn't a flex — it's the experience.

01 The Guest List

Request your invite via the link in our Instagram bio. If your profile is approved, you're on the exclusive free or discounted early guest list.

02 Strict Timing

Invite list closes at 8:00 PM sharp. Arrive even a minute late and your invite is voided. No exceptions. No excuses.

03 Paid Cover

After 8 PM, entry shifts to paid cover at the gate — if the venue hasn't hit full capacity. First come, first served.

04 Gates Close at 9 PM

The gates close permanently at 9 PM. No late-night walk-ins. Period. You're either in or you're not.

05 Dress Code

Mandatory and theme-aligned. Swimwear for sundowners, neon raver gear for After Dark, all-white for property takeovers. No compliance, no entry.
